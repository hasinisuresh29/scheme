Privacy Policy & Terms

Privacy Policy

Scheme Connect is built on a privacy-first foundation. We do not require, collect, or store sensitive identifying credentials such as Aadhaar numbers, biometric data, or banking credentials. Any criteria entered into the interactive Eligibility Checker or Document Checker runs purely client-side on your local device.

Terms & Disclaimer

Scheme Connect is an independent awareness portal. It is not an official government entity and does not charge any fee for accessing scheme information. While every effort is made to maintain verified and up-to-date scheme criteria from official gazettes and portals, citizens are strongly advised to verify details through the relevant official government portal before applying.

Official Government Links

All external links lead directly to official Central and State government domains (.gov.in, .nic.in). Scheme Connect does not act as an agent or intermediary.
